About BBDians Notes

Empowering BBD students with seamless access to academic resources.

BBDians Notes is a student-focused platform created to make academic resources easier to access. Instead of searching across multiple groups or drives, students can find organized notes and PYQs in one place.

The goal of this project is to provide a clean, fast, and simple library of study materials for different branches and semesters. We believe that access to quality study material should be a click away.

This website is currently in its starting phase, and we are continuously working to add more notes and features based on student feedback. If you have any suggestions or want to contribute, please reach out to us through the feedback page.
